# Follow an order

Open **Orders**, then **Order follow-through** on a submitted order or seasonal commitment. No quote is required. The trace joins the current inventory acknowledgment, requested and source promise dates, reconciled Pier quantities, linked commitment and releases, issued invoice and permitted verified card history.

**Refresh order exceptions** reconciles the next-action queue. A lost reservation or stock release acknowledgment retains the original command identity. A confirmed backorder creates one owned shortfall task. Missing inventory, a changed destination or unreconciled quantities remain unknown and cannot close an existing task. Current account and warehouse authority apply to recovery; operations staff do not receive card records.

Each open exception retains an owner, next contact date and source record. Review and save a next-action draft before acting. Saving a draft or contact date sends nothing. An attributed human resolution closes only the inventory exception. A card exception still requires its existing verified provider resolution. The ordinary payment screen retains its separate capture and cancellation approvals.

Carrier delivery and complete settlement remain unknown. Inventory dispatch, a cancellation request, a label, a message, an authorization or resolution of a card task cannot close the whole delivery and payment job. Revised promises, refunds and collection messages retain their separate approvals. Archives carry the reviewed task history, without inventory or payment credentials; restoration initiates no recovery or outreach.
