# Seasonal commitments

Prebooks use the signed-in Barrow command door, `POST /api/barrow/commands`, with a `type` and `payload`. The existing Orders screen shows the deposit acknowledgement, incoming commitment, each ship window and its separate committed, released and outstanding selling quantities. The first version has no dedicated season-publishing or multi-window preparation form; use the authenticated commands below. It does not collect a deposit or allocate money.

An owner or admin publishes immutable terms with `prebook.season`: `id`, `name`, timezone-qualified `opensAt` and `closesAt`, `minimumCents`, `depositBps`, `reason`, and `windows`. Each window has a unique `id`, distinct `requestedDate` and `cancelAfter` calendar dates. Dates are interpreted in Eastern time. The recorded percentage requirement rounds upward once to an integer cent. `GET /api/barrow/prebook-seasons` lists the terms. Publishing does not reserve stock or send notices.

A permitted purchaser calls `order.prepare` with `accountId`, `seasonId`, their PO `reference` and `lines`. Each line chooses the published `requestedDate` and `cancelAfter` along with the usual SKU, selling unit and quantity. Existing brand approval, seller, currency, price, quantity, terms and company approval rules remain in force. The same SKU can occur in two different windows. `order.submit` requires `depositAcknowledged: true` for a nonzero deposit requirement; the Orders checkbox records the same acknowledgement. This is a requirement, never evidence of payment. PAY-003 owns collection, invoice allocations and unapplied funds; ordinary card collection remains limited to a fully reserved issued invoice.

Capacity reads live Pier received stock and dated, unreceived committed purchase orders, subtracting outstanding commercial prebook commitments across seasons and accounts. Each cumulative ship-date deadline must fit. Incoming rows keep the source label “Pier PO promise date”, or “date unknown”; unknown dates cannot establish capacity. Incoming supply is never immediately reserved. Pier supplies no destination warehouse for incoming purchase orders, so restricted-warehouse accounts are refused rather than granted unverified supply. The existing stock observation freshness limit and attributed supplier exception still govern submission. A closed season refuses new preparation and submission, including company approval that would admit a new commitment.

Close intake with `prebook.close`, supplying the season `id`, its current `revision` and a `reason`. Once the season is closed and a window opens, a supplier with existing operations authority calls `prebook.release` with `orderId`, current parent `revision`, `windowId` and a `reason`. The source must now have received stock. The command creates one linked submitted order from the agreed window snapshots. The normal **Approve & reserve stock** control rechecks commercial authority and asks Pier for its durable reservation. Retrying the window returns the same request. A pending or short release remains outstanding; only full Pier acknowledgement counts as released. The child cannot revise its original commitment quantities through the generic quantity-revision door. The current account minimum applies to each released window.

`prebook.delay` uses the same identity and revision fields plus a later `requestedDate` estimate and a `reason`. Its attributed history retains the previous and new estimate. This records a supplier estimate and preserves every agreed line, price, ship date and cancel date. It cannot extend the buyer’s cancel deadline. A delay beyond that deadline refuses release pending a newly reviewed buyer agreement; that agreement flow is not implemented here. No automatic purchase, cancellation, payment or outreach follows a delay.

`GET /api/barrow/orders/<id>/prebook` returns the scoped commitment summary. The fulfillment report adds unassigned `prebooks` with each release link and recorded promise history; a warehouse filter omits these unassigned commitments and a restricted-warehouse report refuses them. The sales report emits original `prebook` booked rows and linked `prebook release` rows with zero additional booked value. Shipment and invoice values come from the released order. Orders in the scoped JSON and order CSV export retain season, prebook metadata, source labels, original windows and release links; owner archives preserve the same records.

The remaining ORD-008 work is the native publishing and multi-window entry flow, an agreed delayed-release amendment, and the collected-deposit journey through the existing card boundary with PAY-003 allocations remaining separate. A recorded deposit requirement and an unpaid commitment must never be described as a collected deposit or a paid invoice.
