# Stock synchronization and requested notices

Connect the real Pier installation using the documented private session-file command in ./scripts/connect-pier.mjs. Pier must authorize the current native supplier session for the intended warehouses. Its manual order routing also needs the owner's reviewed and activated warehouse rule before orders can reserve stock.

In Data, open Stock synchronization and enable scheduled synchronization. The hourly installation job reads up to ten source pages of at most 100 events. Synchronize next page runs one bounded page. A page is recorded atomically before its source acknowledgement; interrupted acknowledgements resume before any later fetch. Stable source event IDs prevent duplicate reconciliation. A checkpoint never advances from fetching alone. Source rate-limit responses defer further requests, and unsuccessful observations never become order promises. Catalog reads and supplier approval still ask Pier for live account-scoped availability.

Buyers open Stock notices in Catalog, choose a product and request a private notice in Barrow. Stock requests are accepted when the product is out of stock. Prebook requests wait for the supplier's recorded notice window to open. Requests are per signed-in buyer, account, SKU and notice kind; Cancel notice withdraws only that request. Current active purchasing membership and assortment are checked again when publishing. Each request is fulfilled once; replay cannot publish it again. These notices do not send email, and say nothing about email delivery. Prebook purchasing, deposits and seasonal publication remain the independent ORD-008 and CAT-008 capabilities; this feature records only the requested notice window.

After three failed synchronization attempts, the installation owner receives an assigned operator follow-up in the same panel. Reconnecting and catching up preserves the open assignment. The operator records an attributed resolution after reconciliation. If Pier permissions or its origin change, record the reason and restart the source checkpoint; retained events and notices are not deleted. A changed native warehouse grant requires this deliberate restart rather than reusing its old source cursor.

Whole archives retain event and notice history, disable synchronization and pending requests. Re-enabling requires an owner action and assigns a new consumer identity. Account and company-user erasure remove their requests and notices, including through the independent restore journal. Supplier commercial exports include stock requests and notices. No source credential enters this history.
