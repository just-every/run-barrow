# Buyer payment promises

Owners, admins and currently entitled account-scoped finance staff can open **Buyer payment promises** in the Data or Reports workspace. Choose the account, an issued invoice and a current buyer. Record integer minor units in the invoice currency, a supplier-calendar due date and the finance decision reason. This records the buyer’s attributed commitment; it sends no message and changes no invoice or payment balance.

Each invoice has one promise record with preserved decision history. Revising records a replacement commitment for remaining principal and takes a new allocation baseline. Earlier allocations cannot satisfy the replacement commitment. Withdrawals, explicit finance resolutions and collection pauses retain actor, time, source revision and the earlier terms. A pause is approved collection metadata, never a provider dispute. Its dated history remains visible after the pause ends.

The hourly scheduler and **Reconcile buyer promises** use the promise’s recorded calendar zone. Due today remains open through the entire day, including daylight-saving changes. The next day escalates the same task, retaining its owner and reviewed next-contact date. If that owner loses current authority, the task returns to a currently authorized supplier owner. Concurrent reconciliation and replay do not create another task or escalation event.

Only matching verified card principal allocations made after the current commitment can satisfy its amount automatically. Partial allocations, an unallocated captured receipt, another invoice’s payment, applied credit, an absent source and a recorded contact do not satisfy it. Finance can instead record an explicit resolution with a reason. Generic follow-up contact and closure controls cannot bypass that decision.

Public commands use `POST /api/barrow/commands` with a `type` and `payload`. Types are `promise.record`, `promise.revise`, `promise.pause`, `promise.withdraw`, `promise.resolve` and `promise.refresh`. Read the current account view from `GET /api/barrow/payment-promises?accountId=ACCOUNT`. Recording requires `accountId`, `orderId`, `sourceRevision`, `buyerId`, `currency`, `amountCents`, `dueDate`, `reason` and `revision: null`. Later decisions require the current promise revision. Revision also needs the current source and replacement terms. Pause takes `pause: { until: "YYYY-MM-DD" }`; `pause: null` ends it. Each decision rechecks current authority and source revisions inside the write transaction.

Finance reports and CSV include source-linked promise state and pause metadata within their entitled account filter. Attributed audit search and CSV retain the decisions. Installation archives retain the history but restore it inactive: reconciliation cannot recreate collection work or close tasks from restored promises. Reviewed account erasure retains numeric financial history and opaque attribution, redacts decision notes and disables the retained promise. An erased account cannot resume collection work.

Automated dunning, contact consent, actual reminder delivery, suppression and provider-dispute handling remain unfinished under AUTO-007. This native promise lifecycle does not establish live provider completion or ACH support.
