## ACC-003 — Billing and delivery locations

## ACC-007 — Buyer purchasing approvals

Implemented semantics (sx c26): company administrators, or the supplier, save versioned approval routes per account. A rule names an amount threshold (orders above it, in USD cents) and ship-to locations (all or named); the most specific rule wins (highest threshold the total exceeds, then a named location over all). A rule has one to five sequential steps; each names one or more current company approvers (active members holding the approver right, checked at save) and may add an escalation time (1–720 hours) with escalation approvers. A matching rule holds a USD order or accepted quote for company approval even when the purchaser’s own limit would let it through; below every rule and the purchaser’s limit ACC-002 is unchanged. The route and its policy revision are frozen on the order, so a later revision never changes a waiting request. Step n opens only after step n−1; a different current person decides each step; the preparer never does; each decider holds the approver right and a limit covering the order. Administrators, buyers without the approver right, agents, the supplier and approvers the route does not name cannot bypass or skip a step. An administrator or the supplier delegates an absent approver to another current approver for a dated window of at most 90 days: the delegate decides only steps naming the delegator, in order, under their own right and limit, recorded as a delegate decision; delegations end on request and whenever either person is removed, erased or loses the right. After a step’s escalation time its escalation approvers may also decide it (the original approvers still can); the request shows its step, approvers, waiting time and escalation in the app, and AUTO-002 notifies the step’s approvers, current delegates and escalation approvers in the app. The submitter or a company administrator withdraws an unapproved request back to a draft, voiding its approvals; revising quantities prepares a replacement, which needs fresh approval at every step. A price, selling-unit or pricing-rule change, an account terms change or an edited or archived ship-to address after submission voids every approval so far and returns the request to a draft with the reason; PAY-004 inherits the same invalidation for credit changes before it ships credit exposure. People named by a saved route cannot be removed, erased or lose the approver right until an administrator edits the route. Supplier approval stays a separate attributable decision. Route saves, each step approval (with delegate or escalation), withdrawals, voiding and delegations are audited.

## PRI-003 — Case packs minimums and increments

## ORD-001 — Quick order and CSV cart upload

Exact semantics (ct 19 Oct 19:01Z): the order pad's **Scan or type SKU** field also matches Pier item barcodes (a keyboard-wedge scanner types into it), and its **Paste or upload CSV** dialog takes pasted lines (`code quantity [unit]`, separated by tabs, commas or spaces) or a CSV; both go through one server preview, the `cart.preview` command, open to the account's buyer, the supplier owner/admin (who act for reps until REP-001 ships) and the account's agent, and never writing a record. A CSV is UTF-8 RFC 4180 with a required header row: `sku` or `barcode` (either per row; both must name the same product) and `quantity`, optional `unit` (a selling unit code; default the base unit) and `buyerSku` (kept as written on the line, never selecting a SKU, per ORD-007); header names ignore case; at most 500 rows and 256 KB. Aliases are Pier barcodes and SKU spelling: a SKU matches exactly, then ignoring case when that is unambiguous, then a barcode matches exactly. Unknown SKUs, SKUs archived in Pier and SKUs on no price list of this account all read the same "not on this account's price list", so nothing about hidden items is revealed. Rows naming the same SKU and unit are combined into one line and the preview names the rows and the sum. PRI-003 rules, stock and pricing apply to each SKU's final quantity (cart plus upload on merge); the preview offers the nearest valid quantities, and the buyer chooses one or leaves the row or line out explicitly; no cart is produced while any row or line is unresolved. Merge adds to the pad's current quantities; Replace makes the pad exactly the upload; the pad then prepares through `order.prepare`, which re-checks every price, rule and stock figure, so the draft total equals the preview total. The working cart downloads (`cart.form`) as CSV in a vertical layout (`sku`, `barcode`, `name`, `unit`, `unitPriceUsd`, `quantity`, one row per variant) or a matrix layout (`style`, `name`, then one column per size, base units only), and both upload back through the same preview; `name` and `unitPriceUsd` are informational and ignored on upload. Completion is judged on these current doors: the line-sheet order form is CAT-003's and the multi-delivery form (per-delivery ship window, PO and ship-to columns, previewed as one release per delivery) is ORD-013's, each inheriting this preview, its explicit merge/replace choice and PRI-003's rules before it ships.

## ORD-002 — Saved shopping lists

Exact semantics: a list belongs to one trade account and keeps a name (1 to 80 characters, unique ignoring case among one person's lists on that account), whether it is shared, its creator and last editor with times, and 1 to 100 lines of SKU, optional selling unit and quantity (above zero, at most six decimal places); it never stores a price, stock figure or promise. At most 50 lists per person per account. A private list is seen only by its creator. A shared list is seen by everyone who can act for that account today: the account's buyer (today's one-buyer company) and the supplier owner/admin, who act for reps until REP-001 ships; ACC-002 inherits sharing with the company users who hold purchasing rights for that account before it ships. Anyone who sees a shared list may edit its name and lines (each save names the editor and needs the current revision); only its creator shares, unshares or deletes it. Duplicating makes a private copy for the person duplicating, on the same account or another account they may act for. `GET /api/barrow/lists?accountId=` lists what the caller may see; the `list.save`, `list.duplicate` and `list.delete` commands are for people only. Agents read shared lists and preview them but never save, share, duplicate or delete. A list on another account, a private list of someone else and an unknown list all read the same "Shopping list not found." Adding a list to a cart is ORD-001's `cart.preview` with the list as its source (the same CSV a buyer could upload, against the list's own account): current account price and pricing rules, live Pier stock and PRI-003 case-pack, increment and minimum corrections apply; a SKU that is unknown, archived in Pier or no longer on the account's price list reads "not on this account's price list" and must be left out explicitly; merge or replace is the buyer's explicit choice, and `order.prepare` rechecks everything, so the draft total equals the preview total. Lists travel in the owner archive and are removed with an erased unused account. No list import ships today: MOVE-001 through MOVE-006 and MOVE-009, if they import lists, each report them in MOVE-007's dry-run reconciliation and add them to DATA-001's export before they ship (ct 20 Oct).

## ORD-005 — Quote conversation revisions and notes

Exact semantics (ORD-005): each quote keeps an append-only thread beside its numbered revisions, with no door that edits or deletes an entry except DATA-003’s owner-reviewed erasure placeholders and attachment removal; entry ID, quote, side, kind, author ID, time and revision remain. Each entry records its author, side, time and the revision current when it was written. Posting never changes the quote’s version, so it cannot invalidate a proposal or approval in progress. ## ORD-015 — Versioned sales terms and buyer acceptance

Exact semantics (sx-barrow-r23, 21 October 2026): a supplier owner/admin publishes immutable numbered versions of a named series (`terms.publish`: a title and up to 20,000 characters of text, a PDF of at most 1 MB, or both; the next version is the next whole number and no command edits or deletes one) and assigns one series to an account (`terms.assign` with `requireAcceptance` true or false, or `series: null` to remove it). An assignment names a series, never a frozen version: the version an account must accept is the series' newest version when `order.submit` runs, and a draft prepared before a new version needs the new one. Sales terms are separate from the account's payment terms and from portal usage terms. Where acceptance is required, `order.submit` refuses without it (`TERMS_REQUIRED`) and refuses a version that is not the newest (`TERMS_VERSION`); an acceptance for an account with no assigned terms is refused (`TERMS_NOT_ASSIGNED`); where acceptance is optional the same inputs are recorded when given and nothing is recorded when not. The account's buyer accepts by naming the version with `accepted: true` (printed name optional). A supplier owner/admin, who acts for reps until REP-001 ships, submits a rep order by naming the version, the buyer's printed name (at most 120 characters) and a PNG signature image drawn on the device (at most about 100 KB); a buyer's signature is not asked for in the portal. The order keeps series, version, title, content digest (SHA-256 of title, text and PDF digest), PDF digest, how (`buyer` or `rep-signature`), who recorded it, when (UTC instant), printed name and signature digest; the signature image is kept by order ID and downloads at `GET /api/barrow/orders/<id>/terms-signature` with exactly the order's own visibility, and the terms PDF downloads for the assigned buyer or the supplier. Each ORD-014 submitted and accepted confirmation snapshot copies the accepted terms text and the acceptance record, so its PDF shows version 1 unchanged after version 2 is published.
