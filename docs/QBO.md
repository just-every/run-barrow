# QuickBooks read-only foundation

The signed-in installation owner connects one QBO company from Settings → QuickBooks accounting. Admins, staff, buyers and agents cannot initiate OAuth, change mappings, read pages or link invoices. Authorized finance viewers can read only their native account's dated comparisons and source observations through the existing statement and finance doors. Functional reconciliation delegation remains unfinished under ACC-005.

Configure optional installation secrets `QBO_CLIENT_ID` and `QBO_CLIENT_SECRET` through the existing deployment secret mechanism. Register this installation's exact HTTPS origin followed by `/api/barrow/qbo/callback` with Intuit. Select sandbox or production explicitly and enter the expected numeric company ID before consent. Client credentials alone establish neither company authority nor tenant consent.

The OAuth accounting scope Intuit offers is broader than read-only. The consent screen discloses that scope. This adapter only sends entity queries to the selected Intuit API host; it never creates, edits or deletes an invoice, credit memo, payment, tax transaction or native commercial record. The callback binds one-use state to the initiating owner session, expected company, environment and ten-minute expiry. It never returns the authorization code or tokens in application JSON.

Access and rotating refresh tokens are sealed with the installation custody key and company-bound associated data. Connection credentials and pending OAuth state are excluded from portable archives. A separate non-secret authority record in the independent R2 journal binds the active connection revision; local revocation changes it before deleting credentials, so D1 rewind cannot reactivate a revoked connection. Secret rotation requires explicit disconnect and fresh consent. Interrupted refresh is visibly inactive and requires reconnecting; restored history cannot initiate reads.

Read Customer, Item, Account and TaxCode reference pages before approving exact source IDs and revisions. Customer mappings bind a native account and its current revision. Item mappings name existing native price-book SKUs. Account and tax-code native labels are reviewed reference metadata only: they confer neither tax approval nor financial write authority. A customer whose financial history was recorded cannot be reassigned to another native account. Changed source reference or account revisions require fresh review.

`qbo.start` records today's supplier-calendar date and a stable correlation. Each explicit `qbo.resume` reads at most 50 invoices and atomically advances that correlation's checkpoint with its observations and audit. An upstream refusal leaves the checkpoint unchanged, records a safe actionable error and exposes the same resume identity in Diagnostics. Reads stop at 100,000 invoices. An unmapped customer refuses the page before any financial observations are stored. Erased customer tombstones are skipped and cannot be mapped again.

Only normalized financial facts are retained: opaque IDs and revisions, native account and order linkage, ISO currency, exact minor units, invoice and due dates, source references, observation instant and latest financial instant. Names, addresses, contact data, line descriptions and provider narratives are discarded at the network boundary. Provider pagination is not a transaction: QBO can change during a multi-page read. Complete observations are dated current balances, never inferred historical balances or verified native payments.

`qbo.link` reviews an existing issued native invoice and refuses a different company, account, currency, total, stale revision or second source identity. It adds source linkage only. It never overwrites an issued native document. A later upstream edit is a visible reconciliation conflict. Linking an invoice does not synchronize payment allocations or credits.

Statements show dated comparisons beside the native closing balance, with matching CSV and PDF rows. Finance shows dated invoice-outstanding comparisons with matching CSV. Each currency retains its own native cents, ledger cents, difference, source IDs, company, observation instant and correlation. Statement cash and invoice outstanding remain distinct. A date without a complete observation shows no provider comparison. Differences never alter native opening or closing balances.

Explicit next-action refresh creates one source-linked owned reconciliation exception per conflicting QBO invoice source revision. A new upstream revision creates fresh owned work even when an earlier revision was acknowledged. Contact attempts and retries do not establish resolution. A fresh matching source observation or an attributed human resolution closes work; an old observation cannot auto-close it.

QBO observation and financial update instants extend the latest-event seven-UTC-calendar-year floor for the native account's orders; leap day clamps to February 28. Existing retention expiry, explicit owner review, legal holds, typed erasure and attributed receipts still apply. Reviewed account revocation removes customer mapping authority. The independent journal carries source tombstones across restoration, preserving required financial facts and refusing renewed reads for erased customers. QBO retains separate provider copies; no provider deletion or legal adequacy is claimed.

Public proof is folded into the existing statement and pricing sanity journeys, using `./tests/api/qbo-accounting.mjs` and `./tests/journeys/qbo-accounting.mjs`. Only the external Intuit network edge is simulated. Native invoices, permissions, audit, statements, finance, erasure and archives use their real public doors.

Full INT-002 remains partial. The local held-key QBO sandbox consent and complete invoice, partial-payment, return-credit, replay and revocation receipt remain required. Invoice creation at selected billing events, credit-memo writes, verified payment and credit synchronization, complete receiving duties and functional reconciliation delegation remain owed. Existing payment and tax holds are unchanged.
