# Barrow rulings

### RL-101 — Reviewed cutover bridge and scheduled-feed authority

**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** MOVE-008, INT-020.

**Question:** May an owner-operated bridge use explicit scoped cutover/delivery/rollback consent and pinned server trust without completing the scheduled SFTP target?

**Adopted/proposed default:** Signed-in owner consents separately to holds, cutoff/pack, each current bridge delivery and native-only rollback; pinned host/server credentials; incumbent history and native stock authorities retained; inert restore.

**Outcome: adopt with rider.** Adopt the bridge's explicit scoped reviewed actions, pinned remote host trust, server-held credentials and no restored replay; define the authoritative system for every open order/invoice and stock fact, and never infer external rollback from native undo. Keep the full scheduled INT-020 feed as owed work, with explicit configuration authorizing its bounded future schedule rather than permanently requiring a manual bridge action per file.

**Why:** NuORDER documents automated per-brand SFTP feeds, documented schemas/templates and two-way order/stock exchange. No common norm settles Barrow's dual hosted/merchant-server topology or exact cutoff authority. Safest default is explicit reviewed migration scope and one source of commercial truth, preserving target automation instead of relabelling a manual bridge as its completion.

**Sources:**
- [NuORDER documents automated per-brand SFTP feeds with standard templates and two-way exchanges.](https://helpdesk.nuorder.com/hc/en-us/articles/16409979437339-NuORDER-Flat-File-SFTP-Integration)

**Placement:** Place the bridge consent/trust/authority boundary once in MOVE-008, cited by INT-020; INT-020 retains its own scheduled-feed acceptance and STK-002/003 inventory/handoff citations.

**Current-main evidence:** CONTRACT.md:301 (clause), :504 (Status row); `e19fc08cb3df98bcb7e8987670c4539249ddb1bb`.

**Engineering/proof boundary:** Scheduled imports, unchanged templates, ATS freshness/account warehouses, expanded data feeds, edit/cancel/shipment exchange and Pier edit command remain engineering gaps. No actual production cutoff/repoint/rollback is authorized; its business owner must sign off when concrete.

### RL-102 — Frozen attribution and non-compensating activity goals

**Date:** 2026-10-28 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** RPT-005.

**Question:** May owner/admin count goals and source-backed performance attribution remain separate from commission entitlement and current account visibility?

**Adopted/proposed default:** Owner/admin explicit count goals; native identity separate from frozen labels; current held-account privacy; no compensation, notification or automatic effect.

**Outcome: adopt with rider.** Adopt dated source-backed performance and explicit non-compensating count goals; keep frozen earned/reversed commission snapshots and native actor identity distinct, label current workload versus historical assignment, and apply current report/account/compensation entitlement before every detail/count/export.

**Why:** NuORDER reporting follows user/company visibility and exposes buyer-submitted versus creator attribution. It does not establish a common exact goal formula or retroactive commission reassignment rule. The safest default keeps historical economic facts immutable while refusing cross-rep private compensation and any implication that goal achievement changes pay.

**Sources:**
- [NuORDER report visibility follows role/company permissions and separates order-creator facts.](https://helpdesk.nuorder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005915306-Reporting-FAQ)
- [NuORDER subteams constrain account/order visibility and retain time-of-order associations.](https://helpdesk.nuorder.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005734306-Subteams-overview-and-FAQ)

**Placement:** State attribution/goal distinction once in RPT-005's own point, citing REP-004 commission snapshots, ACC-006 current account scope and DATA-002 audit/export duties.

**Current-main evidence:** CONTRACT.md:263 (clause), :487 (Status row); `e19fc08cb3df98bcb7e8987670c4539249ddb1bb`.

**Engineering/proof boundary:** Verified payment/credit/refund effects remain PAY-003/PAY-010 receiving duties; no payroll, payout, compensation or status change. Keep all original territory/conversion/new/repeat and itemized-statement acceptance.

### RL-1 — Trade applications and approval

**Date:** 2026-10-22 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** ACC-001.

**Recorded outcome:** A prospective US stockist submits company name, EIN or business identifier where needed, contact, resale documents, requested locations and expected volume. Staff review duplicates and approve, request information or decline with a reason before private prices or terms become accessible. Invitations and application status are visible to both parties. On first sign-in, and again when the merchant publishes a new version, a buyer accepts the current portal usage terms, recorded with version, person and time; ORD-015 governs the separate sales terms on orders. Exact semantics (ct 22 October 2026): the public `/apply` form takes company legal name, optional DBA, EIN (9-digit format check, "not verified with the IRS"; or an explicit no-EIN sole-proprietor choice), contact name/email/phone, requested locations (ACC-003 records and labels), expected annual USD volume and up to five application documents (PDF/PNG/JPEG by bytes, ≤1 MB each, SHA-256), behind CRM-005-style spam checks (honeypot, signed 3 s/24 h form token, 5 per IP-digest per hour, Turnstile only with owner keys) and a required consent line; rejected submissions store nothing. The application stays unverified and invisible to staff until a one-use 10-minute hashed email code (5 attempts, resend invalidates) is entered; unverified submissions are deleted after 24 hours. Verification creates no member, account or price access, only a 24-hour HttpOnly session bound to that application, renewed by a fresh code. Supplier owner/admin and ACC-005 entitled functional reviewers review ACC-004-normalized name, exact EIN and email duplicates against accounts and open applications, bind the reviewed IDs and revisions, never merge, and request information (one upload), approve (native account, ACC-003 locations, invitation requested to the verified email) or decline with a reason; the applicant may withdraw. Applicant and staff see the same state, dates, requests, reasons and documents; internal notes stay staff-only. Application documents require a configured scanner under ct 22 October 15:46Z; without one bounded static uploads and existing documents remain quarantined with no download. Once screening exists, authorized downloads belong only to supplier owner/admin, ACC-005 entitled functional reviewers and the application's own session, as attachments with nosniff; agents are refused. Existing metadata remains labelled not validated (TAX-003) and not malware-screened (DATA-004). Portal usage terms are immutable numbered versions; once one exists every buyer person accepts the newest at first sign-in and after each publish (user, version, SHA-256, time) before any buyer commercial door answers (`USAGE_TERMS_REQUIRED`); agents never accept. Application records join DATA-003 as the "application records" kind (owner-reviewed, never silent deletion; approved applications follow account erasure and the seven-year floor), DATA-001 (`applications`, `applicationdocuments`) and DATA-002; mail uses AUTO-003's sender and labels, development is never sent. REP-007 prospects enter this queue and TAX-003 reviews its certificates before they ship.

### RL-5 — Quick order and CSV cart upload

**Date:** 2026-10-19 · **Ruled by:** ct · **Clauses governed:** ORD-001.

**Recorded outcome:** Buyers and reps add lines by SKU or barcode, paste quantities and upload a CSV with SKU/barcode and quantity. A buyer can also download the working cart as a spreadsheet order form, fill it in offline and upload it back through the same preview. Preview resolves aliases, duplicates, packs, hidden SKUs and errors before replacing or merging a cart by explicit choice. Valid lines are not quietly lost. Uploaded and offline-form quantities inherit PRI-003's case-pack, increment and minimum refusal with explicit corrections. Exact semantics (ct 19 Oct 19:01Z): the order pad's **Scan or type SKU** field also matches Pier item barcodes (a keyboard-wedge scanner types into it), and its **Paste or upload CSV** dialog takes pasted lines (`code quantity [unit]`, separated by tabs, commas or spaces) or a CSV; both go through one server preview, the `cart.preview` command, open to the account's buyer, the supplier owner/admin (who act for reps until REP-001 ships) and the account's agent, and never writing a record. A CSV is UTF-8 RFC 4180 with a required header row: `sku` or `barcode` (either per row; both must name the same product) and `quantity`, optional `unit` (a selling unit code; default the base unit) and `buyerSku` (kept as written on the line, never selecting a SKU, per ORD-007); header names ignore case; at most 500 rows and 256 KB. Aliases are Pier barcodes and SKU spelling: a SKU matches exactly, then ignoring case when that is unambiguous, then a barcode matches exactly. Unknown SKUs, SKUs archived in Pier and SKUs on no price list of this account all read the same "not on this account's price list", so nothing about hidden items is revealed. Rows naming the same SKU and unit are combined into one line and the preview names the rows and the sum. PRI-003 rules, stock and pricing apply to each SKU's final quantity (cart plus upload on merge); the preview offers the nearest valid quantities, and the buyer chooses one or leaves the row or line out explicitly; no cart is produced while any row or line is unresolved. Merge adds to the pad's current quantities; Replace makes the pad exactly the upload; the pad then prepares through `order.prepare`, which re-checks every price, rule and stock figure, so the draft total equals the preview total. The working cart downloads (`cart.form`) as CSV in a vertical layout (`sku`, `barcode`, `name`, `unit`, `unitPriceUsd`, `quantity`, one row per variant) or a matrix layout (`style`, `name`, then one column per size, base units only), and both upload back through the same preview; `name` and `unitPriceUsd` are informational and ignored on upload. Completion is judged on these current doors: the line-sheet order form is CAT-003's and the multi-delivery form (per-delivery ship window, PO and ship-to columns, previewed as one release per delivery) is ORD-013's, each inheriting this preview, its explicit merge/replace choice and PRI-003's rules before it ships.

### RL-8 — Retention erasure and provider custody

**Recorded outcome:** Owners set retention policies for PO documents, media, messages and commercial history, with legal holds and reviewable erasure requests. Expiry is strictly after the configured elapsed 24-hour days and only nominates records for explicit owner review, never silent deletion. A request records requester, channel, verification note, reason, hold, owner and revision. The dry-run lists every current class and individual item, its action, reason and financial-floor date. Execution requires that request, the current account and policy revisions, the unchanged preview, a reason and typed ERASE. It atomically changes native data and import custody with a D1 journal receipt, invalidates transfers and records an audit event; its private write-ahead journal and completion marker live in R2 outside D1. An owner may exclude individual redactions with recorded reasons; access revocation cannot be excluded. Holds block every redaction until attributed owner release, while a separate explicit action may revoke access under a hold. Ordered-account erasure keeps the account and operator IDs, deactivates buyers as “Erased buyer” with unique non-routable `erased-<id>@erased.invalid` addresses, gives the account a different non-sign-in placeholder and closes it while keeping company name, terms, prices, Pier customer ID and other commercial profile. Notice summaries are neutralized; mail event receipts/states are removed, and authenticated later events for an erased delivery are acknowledged without storing their payload. The mail ledger loses recipient, summary personal details and request/response bodies while keeping ID, status, times and provider message ID. Sign-in audit exact email/provider-subject values are replaced; commercial audit keeps every ID, subject, actor, action, time and revision while replacing exact personal values in free text only through erasure, counted in the receipt.
