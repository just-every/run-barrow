# Barrow product contract

Target edition: 17 October 2026. Barrow is the wholesale ordering portal a US brand or wholesaler can use in place of NuORDER by Lightspeed, Pepperi or Brandboom, with the storefront, field-sales and assisted-order depth buyers seek from SparkLayer and WizCommerce. It serves trade buyers, purchasing approvers, sales reps, operations and finance from private catalog discovery through quote, order, delivery, return and payment. USD, US sales tax, resale certificates, ACH and US business time zones are the first-buyer defaults.

This is the product being built towards, not a description limited to today's release. Missing providers, native mobile applications, credit agreements and migrations are engineering work below. The canonical Status table records current implementation separately. Every clause states the buyer behavior and acceptance evidence through a screen, form, command, HTTP API, download or provider event; [ACCEPTANCE.md](ACCEPTANCE.md) lists the same journeys and statuses. Existing BAR-001 through BAR-006 IDs are preserved. BAR-002 extends the former AUD/Pier-only, tax-and-payment-excluded slice to the full US commercial job; legacy records keep their original currency.

Historical currency terminology throughout this contract: “former” and “legacy” AUD refer to orders written before USD currency snapshots were introduced on 19 October 2026 (`src/barrow/orders.ts`, `src/barrow/pier.ts`). Those historical records retain their original currency and cents; new orders use USD.

## Product target

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Category | B2B wholesale ordering portal |
| Incumbents a buyer switches from | NuORDER by Lightspeed, Pepperi, Brandboom; SparkLayer and WizCommerce |
| First buyer | A US sales or ecommerce manager at a brand or wholesaler selling repeatedly to business stockists, with trade purchasers, field reps, operations and a finance lead, in USD |

The complete job includes company approval and purchasing rights, private catalogs and line sheets, negotiated prices, rapid repeat ordering, seasonal prebooks, offline rep selling, terms and credit, processor-backed payment, tax/exemptions, shipping, returns, statements and reconciliation. Barrow owns the buyer/rep experience and commercial follow-through. [Pier](https://github.com/just-every/run-pier) is an optional inventory authority, not a prerequisite product; other ERP/inventory adapters have equal standing. [Pantry](https://github.com/just-every/run-pantry) and connected accounting systems own the general ledger; [Nectar](https://github.com/just-every/run-nectar) remains the separate subscription/usage billing product; [Orbit](https://github.com/just-every/run-orbit) remains the general CRM. Barrow's wholesale cart quotes, account history and receivables visibility serve its own ordering job and link to those products rather than absorbing them.

## Operating rules

Current source and tests are judged at the commit in Status. A source citation establishes only the implemented portion named there; local tests at a provider boundary are not evidence of live merchant enrollment or carrier execution. New work must prove real provider sandbox or controlled network-edge journeys before a clause becomes done. No mock application, invented stock, inferred bank settlement or silent connector fallback can fulfill this contract.

Accounts, legal sellers, currencies, source authorities and revisions are explicit. Catalogs and commercial documents are private by default. Human approvals bind to the exact proposed effects; agents do not acquire purchasing, credit or refund authority from text in a PO. Imported history cannot trigger live stock, payment or outreach effects. Stale or failed handoffs remain visible with an owner until acknowledged or deliberately resolved.

## Published guarantees carried forward

- **BAR-001 — Accounts and access.** Every product HTTP handler authenticates the current native session or a revocable agent grant. Supplier actions require owner/admin; current customer access is bound to the exact account email. Browser mutations enforce the installation origin and writes recheck current SQL authority. An email edit cannot transfer an existing account or its orders. Company and rep permissions added below must retain this server-side isolation. **Acceptance evidence:** Using the native sign-in and HTTP commands, read as two different buyers, attempt foreign account reads, cross-origin writes and supplier actions as a buyer, then revoke an agent grant. Unauthorized requests return no commercial data and perform no mutation.

- **BAR-002 — Orders and invoices.** Orders retain exact price, currency, terms and human approval snapshots, and send one durable idempotent command to the chosen inventory authority. Pier remains authoritative when selected. Shortfalls remain explicit and cannot become fully fulfilled invoices; invoicing a permitted split names only its allocated or shipped units. USD integer cents are the US default; legacy AUD records retain their currency. Tax, shipping and collection extend this order record through the clauses below rather than remaining exclusions. Completion is judged on today’s whole-order Pier handoff and invoices: only orders whose entire quantities are acknowledged as reserved are invoiceable; pending and shortfall orders cannot be exported as invoices. ORD-009 inherits eligible-quantity split invoicing before introducing permitted splits. INT-001, INT-004 through INT-010 and INT-017 through INT-020 inherit the exact snapshots, single durable handoff, acknowledgment and shortfall obligations before adding another inventory authority. PAY-001 through PAY-012, TAX-001 through TAX-004 and SHIP-001 through SHIP-005 extend the linked commercial record while preserving its original agreed currency, exact lines, terms and attributed approvals before their respective payment, tax and shipping capabilities ship. **Acceptance evidence:** Prepare and approve a USD order, lose the inventory response and repeat the command; inspect one external reservation, an explicit shortfall, and an invoice limited to eligible quantities. Change current prices afterward and download again: the issued snapshot and currency are unchanged. Repeat for a legacy AUD record.

- **BAR-003 — Agent preparation.** Revocable seven-day credentials read catalogs and prepare validated drafts, recording their grant identity. People submit and approve. Agents cannot approve, submit, export invoices, import, erase or issue grants. Broader account agents below use this draft-and-human-authority boundary for commercial effects. **Acceptance evidence:** Issue a grant through the owner door, use the documented catalog and prepare-order commands, attempt each prohibited operation, revoke the grant and repeat the read. Inspect the recorded preparing, submitting and approving identities.

- **BAR-004 — Account move-in.** Base import-kit retains reviewed trade-account CSV plans, original source custody, fingerprints, resumable jobs and undo journals. Source/account changes invalidate approval. Native edits, identity reassignment and order dependencies block unsafe undo; replay resumes the same job. Named platform importers below extend this door. **Acceptance evidence:** Upload the documented account/price CSV through Move in, preview without account writes, refuse a stale fingerprint, apply over several batches and replay it. Inspect originals and journal, then undo untouched accounts and refuse undo after a native edit or dependent order.

- **BAR-005 — Export, restore and erasure.** Owner archives restore commercial records and import custody while excluding authentication, agent and connection credentials. Erasing an unused account retires import custody and atomically commits the portability redaction checkpoint. A later erasure journal fences an older archive. Ordered accounts retain commercial history; identity, inventory and provider custody remain separate and are disclosed. **Acceptance evidence:** Export a populated installation through its portability door, restore into an empty installation and compare commercial records. Confirm credentials are excluded. Erase an unused imported account, export again, and refuse the older archive when supplied with the later journal; refuse erasure of an account with orders.

- **BAR-006 — Run site.** The separate public site introduces Barrow, links to /run and serves repository owner guides at /docs without reading installation data. Installed / remains the Lobby. The existing deployment gate controls publication. Target promises are distinct from the implementation status published by this contract. **Acceptance evidence:** Build with rer build --site, serve the output and traverse the homepage, /run, guide navigation, local assets and a missing guide. Confirm the installed route keeps its Lobby and the site contains no private account data.

## Target clauses — 17 October 2026

### Trade accounts and authority

- **ACC-001 — Trade applications and approval.** A prospective US stockist submits company name, EIN or business identifier where needed, contact, resale documents, requested locations and expected volume. Staff review duplicates and approve, request information or decline with a reason before private prices or terms become accessible. Invitations and application status are visible to both parties. On first sign-in, and again when the merchant publishes a new version, a buyer accepts the current portal usage terms, recorded with version, person and time; ORD-015 governs the separate sales terms on orders. **Acceptance evidence:** Apply from the public trade form, request a missing certificate, approve and sign in, accepting the portal usage terms. The new buyer sees only its approved assortment; a declined or pending applicant cannot see prices, and publishing new usage terms requires acceptance at the next sign-in.

- **ACC-002 — Company buyers and purchasing rights.** Each company has a company administrator who manages contacts and locations, purchasers who build carts, purchasing approvers who release allowed orders and finance viewers who read invoices/statements. Rights and spend limits are explicit per user and location. Company administrators cannot grant supplier powers or access another company. Before it ships, any import this clause adds or feeds reports its entities in MOVE-007’s dry-run reconciliation (source against planned counts and sums by currency, rejected fields, missing entities) and records the approved fingerprint. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite four company users, build a cart as a limited purchaser, approve it as the authorized approver, read its statement as finance and refuse finance ordering and cross-company administration.

- **ACC-003 — Billing and delivery locations.** An account maintains separate bill-to, ship-to and contact records with US address validation, default delivery instructions and location-specific tax exemption references. Orders snapshot the chosen addresses; an address edit never rewrites an already accepted order. Before it ships, this clause adds its records to DATA-001’s export (JSON and per-kind CSV) with stable IDs, currency, source mapping and revision, in the same owner/buyer scope. Before it ships, any import this clause adds or feeds reports its entities in MOVE-007’s dry-run reconciliation (source against planned counts and sums by currency, rejected fields, missing entities) and records the approved fingerprint. **Acceptance evidence:** Save a headquarters and two stores, place an order to each store, change the default afterward and inspect unchanged order addresses and the correct tax jurisdictions.

- **ACC-004 — Account lifecycle and commercial profile.** Staff maintain account identifiers, active/on-hold/closed states, channel, territory, assigned rep, catalogs, negotiated terms and provider references. Deactivation blocks new orders while buyers retain permitted history and statements. Duplicate companies are reviewed without silently reassigning historical ownership. Until ACC-005 supplies functional supplier roles, the owner or admin maintains this profile. Legacy accounts without a state are active. On-hold and closed accounts cannot prepare a new draft, submit a draft or receive a first supplier approval; history, invoices and recovery of an already approved reservation remain available. Channel, territory, rep, catalog and provider references are recorded profile metadata; ACC-005, ACC-006, CAT-006 and CAT-008 add their scoped role, assignment and catalog capabilities before shipping them. A matching company name after NFKC normalization, trimming, whitespace collapsing and lowercasing, or an exact Pier customer ID, is a possible duplicate. The supplier reviews the matching account IDs and current revisions as separate companies before a native create or company-name/Pier-customer-ID change. CSV previews bind native and source-company matches into the reviewed fingerprint; applying it records the review actor and time and never merges identities. CSV updates preserve existing lifecycle and profile metadata absent from that format. Completion covers current account, ordering, history and invoice doors; PAY-007 must preserve authorized statement access for on-hold and closed accounts before introducing statements. **Acceptance evidence:** Record the commercial profile, place an order, hold then close its account and attempt checkout through the buyer and agent HTTP doors, including submission and first approval of earlier drafts. History and invoices remain readable to the same authorized buyer; new orders are refused and a duplicate email cannot take over the company. Recover an already-approved lost reservation response after closure without a second stock command. Refuse an unreviewed or stale duplicate-company save, review separate companies, import a duplicate-bearing CSV plan and inspect the attributed review through an owner export; original identities and native profile fields survive.

- **ACC-005 — Supplier roles and separation of duties.** Owner manages installation and grants; sales managers control catalogs, prices, assignments and exceptions; reps sell only assigned accounts; operations staff manage fulfillment and returns; finance controls credit, refunds and reconciliation; analysts read authorized reports. Permissions apply equally to screens, API, exports and background work, with amount limits for exceptions. This clause inherits DATA-002 append-only, searchable and exportable attributed audit events for permission/role decisions, including actor/source/time/revision/reason, before its capability ships. Before shipping, operations-role access inherits RPT-003’s report and its account/warehouse scope obligations. Before shipping, this clause inherits PRI-007 scoped manager exception authority and limits, with attributed reasons and audit. **Acceptance evidence:** Invite each supplier role, attempt a credit override as a rep and a catalog edit as operations, allow the authorized finance and manager actions and inspect attributed audit events.

- **ACC-006 — Rep territories and delegated coverage.** Managers assign reps to named accounts and territories with temporary coverage dates. Buyers see their current rep, and a transfer changes future access and attribution without rewriting previous orders or commissions. **Acceptance evidence:** Assign two reps disjoint accounts, give one temporary vacation coverage and expire it. Order and export access follows current assignments; historical commission attribution survives a transfer.

- **ACC-007 — Buyer purchasing approvals.** Company administrators set location or amount approval policies, multiple sequential approvers, delegation and escalation. A purchaser can revise or withdraw an unapproved request; a material price, quantity, address or credit change invalidates its approval. Supplier approval remains a separately attributable decision. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit a $6,000 cart above a purchaser limit, complete two approvals, revise quantities and require fresh approval. Delegate an absent approver and prove a lower-rights buyer cannot bypass the route.

### Catalogs and merchandising

- **CAT-001 — Private account assortments.** Only active SKUs and agreed prices assigned to the signed-in account appear in its catalog or ordering responses. Another account cannot retrieve hidden SKUs, prices or the inventory authority credentials by guessing identifiers. **Acceptance evidence:** Sign in as two buyers with different price lists and request both catalogs and direct API paths; each sees only its own priced items and no upstream credentials.

- **CAT-002 — Product discovery and media.** Buyers search SKU, barcode, product name and description, filter by category, season, availability and attributes, and inspect images, zoom, specifications and downloadable product attachments. Private media follows account permissions and search remains useful in a catalog of at least 10,000 variants: on that fixture a barcode or SKU lookup returns its variant, and a filtered search returns its first page, within 1 second at the 95th percentile. Before it ships, this clause adds its records to DATA-001’s export (JSON and per-kind CSV) with stable IDs, currency, source mapping and revision, in the same owner/buyer scope. Before it ships, any import this clause adds or feeds reports its entities in MOVE-007’s dry-run reconciliation (source against planned counts and sums by currency, rejected fields, missing entities) and records the approved fingerprint. **Acceptance evidence:** Load a 10,000-variant wholesale assortment fixture, find a known SKU by barcode within 1 second and by attribute filters, view its images and spec sheet, and refuse the same private attachment from an unentitled buyer.

- **CAT-003 — Digital line sheets.** Merchandisers assemble seasonal visual line sheets with product variants, wholesale prices and, where the sheet is set to show them, suggested retail prices (PRI-008), packs, availability and ship windows. Each sheet chooses wholesale only, retail only or both. Buyers add lines directly to a cart; account-scoped sharing and PDF downloads retain the recipient price book, price display setting and revision. Sending a sheet to many buyers at once, with segments, suggested-products order forms and campaign tracking, is CAT-011. A sheet's downloadable order form inherits ORD-001's cart preview (aliases, duplicates, hidden SKUs, PRI-003 corrections, explicit merge or replace) before it ships. **Acceptance evidence:** Publish a spring line sheet showing wholesale and MSRP to one account and a wholesale-only sheet to another account with different prices, order from each on a phone and export each account PDF. Each page and PDF shows exactly its configured price columns; a revoked share cannot expose the sheet or another account price.

- **CAT-004 — Shoppable lookbooks and presentations.** Staff combine editorial images, video, product hotspots and collections into branded lookbooks and rep presentations. Every hotspot opens an eligible live product and ordering respects current commercial rules even when the presentation is older. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a lookbook with a video and three hotspots, share to an approved buyer, retire one SKU and order from the remaining two; the retired item is clearly unavailable and historical presentation does not bypass pricing.

- **CAT-005 — Branded responsive storefront.** A wholesaler configures its logo, colors, domain, collections and navigation for a trade storefront with sign-in, clear support contact and account-aware merchandising. Responsive layout preserves shopping and checkout on desktop and phone. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a brand, sign in and complete a representative cart at 1440px and 390px. Logo, navigation, prices, validation and the final confirmation remain readable and operable.

- **CAT-006 — Multiple brands and buying profiles.** One installation can present separate branded storefronts, assortments and price books while an authorized buyer switches purchasing profiles deliberately. Legal seller, currency, permissions and order ownership remain visible and a cart cannot accidentally mix sellers. Additional brands inherit PRI-001: explicit ISO currency labels and exact integer minor units without conversion by relabeling; whole-unit quantities × integer cents need no rounding. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure two brands and a buyer approved for one, refuse the other brand, then approve a second profile and switch. Each cart, invoice and export names the correct seller and ISO currency, retaining exact integer minor units without conversion by relabeling.

- **CAT-007 — Style color size and assortment matrices.** Products support style/color/size variants, barcodes, images, size runs and fixed assortment packs. A matrix order resolves into exact variant SKUs, with variant-specific stock and minimum rules rather than a single ambiguous style quantity. Wholesale price and MSRP may differ per size within one style (for example extended sizes), and the matrix shows each size's price. **Acceptance evidence:** Order a size run that includes a higher-priced extended size and a case assortment through a line-sheet matrix, inspect exact SKU quantities and per-size prices in the order and inventory handoff, and reject an unavailable size without silently reallocating it.

- **CAT-008 — Season and catalog publishing.** Merchandisers stage imports and edits, preview an account view, schedule effective seasons and retire products without losing historical lines. Retiring or canceling a product that still sits in open orders goes through the ORD-016 preview rather than leaving those orders unchanged. Published revisions and archived media references remain identifiable on orders and presentations. **Acceptance evidence:** Stage next-season products, preview as a named buyer, publish at its effective date and archive a prior style. New orders use the new assortment; prior orders keep their original SKU description. Retiring a style with an open order opens the ORD-016 preview for it.

- **CAT-009 — Bulk product and catalog maintenance.** Authorized staff import and export catalog spreadsheets with SKU/variant keys, attributes, media references, categories and assortment assignments. Preview identifies invalid rows, duplicated variants and removed listings before explicit apply. The selected ERP may remain the product authority. Before it ships, any import this clause adds or feeds reports its entities in MOVE-007’s dry-run reconciliation (source against planned counts and sums by currency, rejected fields, missing entities) and records the approved fingerprint. **Acceptance evidence:** Preview a product workbook containing a duplicate barcode and broken media reference, correct it, apply and export. A source-owned field edit is refused or explicitly routed to that source.

- **CAT-010 — Bulk image upload and naming rules.** Authorized staff drag in batches of up to 1,000 product images, or a zip archive, at once. A configurable naming rule (for example style + color + position suffix, or barcode + position) links each file to its style, color or variant and image slot. Preview lists matched files with their target and position and unmatched files with the reason: unknown style, malformed name, unsupported type or oversize. Unmatched files are held and attach automatically when a matching product is imported later. Re-uploading a file for the same slot replaces it in place, one image per slot, and identical content is stored once. Images are private media under CAT-002 permissions and flow to line sheets, lookbooks, PDFs and the rep app's offline cache. **Acceptance evidence:** Configure a style_color_position rule and drag in a batch that includes a misspelled filename and an image for a style not yet imported, then import that style and re-upload one image. Matched files attach to the right variant and position, the misspelling is listed with its reason, the held file attaches after the import, and the re-upload replaces its slot without a duplicate.

- **CAT-011 — Line sheet group sends and campaigns.** From a published line sheet, lookbook, custom product list or working order, an authorized user (within ACC-005 role rights and ACC-006 rep assignment) sends it to many buyers in one action. Recipients are individual approved buyers or a saved segment defined by ACC-004 and ACC-006 attributes: channel, territory, assigned rep, season activity, last-season order value or tag. A segment resolves at send time to approved buyers only; pending, declined, held and closed accounts are excluded and listed as excluded with the reason. The user writes a subject and message, can attach each recipient's own priced PDF or XLSX, and can choose to send a suggested-products order form: pre-filled lines and quantities the buyer adjusts and confirms through the normal cart under PRI-007 and ORD-015 rules. The user can send a test to themselves, save the send as a draft and schedule it. Each recipient gets a personal link delivered through INT-015 that is priced and scoped to their own account. Opening it requires that buyer's sign-in or a single-account signed token, so a forwarded link never shows another account's prices. Delivery, bounce and suppression state is kept per recipient. The send is kept as a campaign record with delivered, bounced, suppressed, opened, viewed and ordered counts and order value per recipient; orders are attributed to it under RPT-002's rules. Resending a campaign reaches only recipients who have not yet received it, and adding accounts to it sends only to the new ones. Reps do the same for their assigned accounts from the rep app (REP-001). **Acceptance evidence:** Build a segment of 30 accounts across two price books that includes one pending account, one closed account and one suppressed email address. Send a spring line sheet to it as a suggested-products order form with a PDF attached, after a test send and a saved draft. Exactly 28 sends go out, the pending and closed accounts are listed as excluded and the suppressed address is reported as suppressed. Open the link as buyers from each price book: each sees only their own prices on the page and in the PDF. Forward one link to a buyer at another account, who gets no access to the first account's prices. Place two orders from the form: the campaign shows 28 delivered, the opens and views, and 2 orders with their value. Resend the campaign: no new email reaches any of the 28 recipients.

### Commercial pricing

- **PRI-001 — Negotiated USD price books.** US accounts receive negotiated price books in USD with exact integer minor units, visible currency and documented rounding. Integer-cent prices multiplied by whole-unit quantities require no rounding; screens label the ISO currency code. New orders record USD; orders without a currency field are legacy AUD and retain that label and their cents through restore. Existing AUD orders retain their original currency; currency is never inferred from a dollar symbol or converted by relabeling an amount. PRI-008 adds optional suggested retail prices (MSRP) per currency beside the wholesale price and inherits these exact-cent and visible-currency obligations before it ships. CAT-006 and PRI-006 inherit them for additional brands and currencies. **Acceptance evidence:** Assign different USD prices to two accounts, prepare orders and compare checkout, invoice and exported cents. Restore a legacy AUD order and show AUD unchanged.

- **PRI-002 — Quantity breaks and account discounts.** Price books express volume or graduated breaks, account/group discounts and approved overrides with an explicit precedence and effective period. Cart and quote explain which rule won; changing quantity recomputes the full eligible total before approval. Current cart, selling-unit, agent-draft, reorder and order/invoice doors conform; ORD-004 and ORD-005 inherit the same pricing explanations and frozen rule snapshots before adding quotes or quote revisions. Until ACC-005 supplies supplier roles and exception limits, the signed-in owner/admin approves rules by saving the account; agents cannot edit them. Each SKU stock-unit price and each configured selling-unit price may have its own dated rule; thresholds count that selected selling unit, never mixed-unit stock totals. Effective dates are inclusive YYYY-MM-DD days in America/New_York. A volume threshold applies its rate to the whole line at or above the threshold; graduated rates apply only to quantities above each threshold, with the agreed rate below the first. A named group discount is assigned in the account price; an explicit account discount, including zero, replaces it. An approved override with a reason replaces all breaks and discounts. Rates are integer USD cents and discounts integer basis points; the exact line amount after discount rounds once to nearest cent, half up. Orders sum integer line cents. The cart recomputes when quantities change; submission and first approval refuse a changed effective rule and require a new draft. Orders, invoice CSV and archives freeze the applied rule, segments, date, discount, explanation and exact total; legacy CSV account imports preserve rules absent from their format. This clause inherits DATA-002 append-only, searchable and exportable attributed audit events for discount and override approvals, including actor/source/time/revision/reason, before its capability ships. **Acceptance evidence:** Price quantities just below and above a volume break, combine an account discount and a negotiated override, and verify the displayed rule and exact total survive the order snapshot.

- **PRI-003 — Case packs minimums and increments.** Merchants set SKU minimums, order-value minimums, case-pack multiples and allowed increments, including per-split minimum policies. Invalid quantities receive an explanation and an explicit suggested correction; no import silently inflates a buyer commitment. Exact semantics: each account price row may set `casePack` and `increment` (whole base units, at most 10,000) beside its minimum, through the account form, the `account.save` API and the reviewed account CSV (`casePack`,`increment` columns after `city`,`state`); a rule set that allows no quantity is refused. A SKU's combined base quantity across its selling units is valid only when it is at least the minimum, a whole multiple of the case pack, and the minimum plus a whole number of increments. The order pad, the `order.prepare` API, agent drafts and reorder review refuse an invalid quantity naming each broken rule and the nearest valid quantities at or below (when one meets the minimum) and above; nothing is corrected for the buyer, who accepts a correction only by choosing it (the pad's **Use N** button or a resent quantity). Completion is judged on these current doors: the cart CSV step is ORD-001's and per-split minimums are ORD-009's, each inheriting these rules before it ships, and MOVE-001 through MOVE-009 map source case packs and increments onto them. **Acceptance evidence:** Enter below-minimum and non-case quantities in the cart, CSV and API, then split a valid order. Each door enforces the configured rules and requires the buyer to accept any corrected quantities.

- **PRI-004 — Unit of measure pricing.** Buyers select allowed each/case/pallet or measured units with exact conversions, price basis, pack contents and stock deductions. Mixed units cannot create fractional cases accidentally and invoice lines name both ordered units and base units. The current account price is per one Pier stock unit; optional, case-sensitive selling-unit definitions add a distinct code, exact base-quantity conversion, integer USD-cent price and pack contents. Counts, including case and pallet, are whole units. Explicit measured units accept positive quantities and conversions with up to six decimal places and at most 1,000,000; conversions requiring finer base precision or exceeding the inventory authority range are refused. Each measured line rounds once to the nearest cent, exact half cents up, and the order adds those integer line cents. Combined base quantities per SKU enforce minimums and stock and become one Pier reservation line per item. Orders and invoice CSV retain ordered unit, conversion, base unit/quantity, pack contents and line-price basis; old records leave unrecorded unit labels absent. Draft editing and ORD-003 reorders preserve separate SKU/unit lines and revalidate current conversions and prices; issued snapshots never change. Later order doors in ORD-001, ORD-002, ORD-004, ORD-008, ORD-009, ORD-013, ORD-014, REP-002 and INT-012 inherit these unit and price-basis obligations before shipping. **Acceptance evidence:** Order two cases of 12 and six each of one SKU, inspect 30 base units in the handoff and the correct separate invoice bases, and reject an undefined unit conversion.

- **PRI-005 — Bundles and wholesale promotions.** Staff create fixed bundles, mix-and-match offers and time-limited promotions with eligible accounts, SKU groups, redemption rules and stacking precedence. Preview exposes the discount and margin impact; returns reverse only the original applicable discount. **Acceptance evidence:** Combine eligible styles to qualify for a mix-and-match promotion, remove one, apply a nonstackable discount and return part of the bundle. Totals and credits follow the published rule once.

- **PRI-006 — Effective prices and additional currencies.** Merchants schedule price-book revisions and, after the USD default, offer explicit CAD/EUR/GBP books where enabled. One order has one explicitly labeled ISO currency; all prices and totals retain exact integer minor units (whole-unit quantity × integer cents requires no rounding), taxes and provider collections use it, and reports disclose their recorded USD conversion basis rather than rewriting transaction amounts. **Acceptance evidence:** Schedule a future USD revision, quote before and after it, then place a CAD order through a compatible payment sandbox. All documents keep the agreed ISO currency and original integer minor units and the report shows its dated USD conversion; whole-unit quantities × integer cents need no rounding.

- **PRI-007 — Explainable commercial validation.** The server revalidates account entitlement, price revision, minimums, units, stock freshness, credit, tax and shipping before acceptance. Buyers see each blocking rule and whether a manager can approve an exception. Stale client totals and agent-provided prices cannot become authoritative. Completion is judged on current admission rules (ct 19 October 2026): prepare reads live Pier and calculates account prices, SKU/order minimums, PRI-003 packs/increments and exact selling-unit conversions/aggregate base stock; client and agent prices, currency, terms and totals are never authority. Submit rechecks the current account/lifecycle/revision, effective pricing, complete cart against live Pier and STK-002 freshness policy; first supplier approval rechecks account and live Pier commercial eligibility, then the durable reservation acknowledges exact live quantities or a visible shortfall. A revoked/unreachable inventory connection blocks admission without losing the draft/submission. Price, unit, minimum and account failures require a corrected, newly prepared draft; they have no bypass. Only stale stock has an exception today: owner/admin records a reason on that draft, audited under STK-002, and reservation still uses live Pier. PAY-004 and PAY-005 inherit credit-exposure validation, the credit-exhaustion-after-drafting acceptance step and authorized recorded credit exceptions; TAX-001, TAX-002 and TAX-003 inherit tax/exemption validation; SHIP-001 inherits shipping validation; ACC-005 inherits scoped manager authority and limits before those capabilities ship. **Acceptance evidence:** Alter client and agent totals and prices, refuse invalid units, packs and minimums, change a price after drafting and submit through the public API. Recheck live stock and refuse an unavailable/revoked source; a supplier reservation shortfall stays visible and cannot invoice. Exercise freshness refusal, refresh and a supplier-only reasoned exception, and inspect its audit. The credit-exhaustion step is retained in PAY-004/PAY-005 before credit ships.

- **PRI-008 — Suggested retail prices and price display.** Price books retain exact integer minor units and explicit ISO currency labels under PRI-001, and carry an optional suggested retail price (MSRP) per variant and currency next to the wholesale price; it is imported, edited and exported with the price book and may differ by size. Merchants choose for each line sheet, lookbook, storefront and document type whether buyers see wholesale, retail or both, and where both show, the buyer sees the margin between them. MSRP is informational only: it never feeds cart, quote, tax, invoice, payment or commission totals, and a missing MSRP shows as absent rather than derived from wholesale. **Acceptance evidence:** Import a price book with wholesale and MSRP columns in USD and CAD, publish one line sheet showing both prices and one showing wholesale only, and open each as its buyer on the page, PDF, order confirmation (ORD-014) and order CSV. Each shows exactly the configured columns and ISO currency with exact integer minor units, the displayed margin matches the two prices, and every order total equals the wholesale lines alone. Whole-unit quantities × integer cents require no rounding.

### Buying quoting and order lifecycle

- **ORD-001 — Quick order and CSV cart upload.** Buyers and reps add lines by SKU or barcode, paste quantities and upload a CSV with SKU/barcode and quantity. A buyer can also download the working cart as a spreadsheet order form, fill it in offline and upload it back through the same preview. ## Push further with account agents

- **AGT-001 — Turn inbound purchase orders into validated drafts.** An account agent reads authorized buyer email, PDF, spreadsheet and photographed/handwritten PO images, maps buyer aliases to SKUs and extracts quantities, units, addresses, references and dates with source-page/region citations and confidence. It validates against current account catalogs, prices, stock and terms, asks for missing details through a human-reviewed draft message and prepares an order for buyer/rep approval. Documents are untrusted data and cannot grant tools or change policy. **Acceptance evidence:** Submit email, PDF and photographed POs containing an ambiguous SKU and a hostile instruction. Review cited extraction and validation errors, correct the ambiguity and approve a draft. Nothing is ordered or sent before the authorized person approves.

- **AGT-002 — Resolve substitution and stock exceptions.** Using original PO lines, account assortment, approved substitutes, units, current ATP and commercial constraints, the agent proposes ranked substitutions or split windows with exact price/tax/freight differences. Buyer/rep approves the basket and a manager approves any commercial exception; changed stock or revisions invalidate the proposal. **Acceptance evidence:** Send a PO for a discontinued style, inspect two grounded substitutes and delivery options, decline one and approve a revised basket. Change inventory before execution and require revalidation; no substitute ships without the required approval.

- **AGT-003 — Prepare a credit-hold resolution.** The agent joins the account balance, open invoices/orders, provider payment state, credit policy and buyer correspondence to explain a hold and propose payment allocation, deposit or finance review. Finance approves any override and the buyer approves a payment mandate; the agent cannot raise a limit, create lender approval, charge or forgive debt by itself. **Acceptance evidence:** Create a held order with an unsettled ACH payment and disputed invoice. Inspect a source-linked resolution plan, approve a deposit path and let finance release the hold only after verified events satisfy policy.

- **AGT-004 — Explainable sell-through reorder plans.** With buyer-authorized sell-through from Shopify POS or Lightspeed Retail (X-Series) exports or APIs, other POS exports or a connected inventory feed, purchase cadence, seasonality, stock-on-hand, open orders and budget, the agent proposes a timed replenishment basket and explains each quantity. Absent sell-through is labeled unknown rather than invented. Buyer chooses quantities/date, rep controls contact cadence and no unattended purchase occurs. **Acceptance evidence:** Load a stockist sales/stock feed with an outage and open prebook, compare the proposed basket to actual demand and commitments, edit it and approve once. Show uncertainty for the outage and measure accepted, delivered and paid outcomes.

- **AGT-005 — Follow every order to delivery and payment.** The account agent joins order/release acknowledgments, promised dates, inventory fulfillment, carrier events, invoices and provider settlements, opens owned exceptions and drafts appropriate follow-ups until both delivery and payment are verified or a human records resolution. Revised promises, cancellation, refunds and collection messages require human approval; a label, sent message or bank pending state never closes the job. **Acceptance evidence:** Lose an inventory acknowledgment, delay one parcel and return an ACH debit. Inspect a source-linked action queue, approve outreach and resolve each real event; closure requires the verified delivery/settlement or an explicit attributed human exception.

- **AGT-006 — Reconcile a wholesale portal cutover.** An agent compares retained incumbent exports with target accounts, variants, price books, locations, open orders, releases and receivables, proposes mappings and highlights count/value differences with source citations. The owner approves mappings and balances before activation; the agent never rewrites ledger authority or sends imported orders to providers automatically. **Acceptance evidence:** Give the agent a real-shaped incumbent workbook and ledger export with duplicated company names and a missing release. Inspect proposed mappings and reconciled totals, correct one mapping and sign off before applying. Native edits continue to fence undo.

- **AGT-007 — Plan accountable account follow-up.** The agent uses actual quote conversations, abandoned carts, order cadence, visits, buyer preferences and unresolved service/payment tasks to propose the next useful rep action and a grounded message. Reps review recipient/content/timing, can suppress outreach and see completion outcomes rather than treating generated text as closed work. **Acceptance evidence:** Present an account with a delayed delivery and an unanswered quote. Review a plan that addresses the delivery before proposing more stock, approve one message and suppress another; unanswered work stays assigned until the buyer responds or a rep resolves it.

- **AGT-008 — Approval-bound agent execution and provenance.** Every agent proposal records source data, tool permissions, model/run identity, freshness, proposed effects and human approvals, with account-scoped revocation and cost/contact limits. External effects bind to the approved revision and idempotency key; stale data, revoked authority or prompt-injected documents stop execution and preserve an inspectable reason. **Acceptance evidence:** Approve a substitution proposal, change its source price and revoke its grant before execution. Refuse the stale/revoked effect, show its provenance and approval in history and execute a newly reviewed revision once after reauthorization.

## Honest boundaries

Barrow is a complete wholesale buyer/rep product, not an inventory ERP, WMS, general ledger, consumer storefront, general CRM, subscription biller or retailer marketplace. It connects to the buyer's existing authorities through INT-001 to INT-020; the integration and buyer-facing order status remain Barrow's responsibility. Wholesale-native quotes, returns and account follow-up remain in scope. Inventory movements, physical picking, manufacturing scheduling and carrier operations happen in their named systems, with acknowledged state returned to Barrow.

Stripe operates card/ACH rails; Resolve operates its financing/credit decisions. The merchant contracts and enrolls with those providers, retains its accounts and receives clearly disclosed provider fees and eligibility. Barrow builds the consent, mandate, tokenization, refund, dispute, reconciliation and enrollment flows rather than excluding payment or terms. Avalara/TaxJar maintain tax content; ECM supports certificate custody. The merchant and its tax advisor own nexus policy and filings, while Barrow calculates, records and exports the provider-backed transaction trail. EasyPost and USPS/UPS/FedEx carry parcels; freight partners quote and move LTL. Barrow records their actual events and owns unresolved buyer exceptions.

Native iOS/Android offline rep applications are part of REP-002 and REP-003, including secure local custody, signing/distribution and device permissions. Provider app registrations, OAuth approvals, US financing agreements and EDI partner onboarding are delivery obligations, not scope reductions. Broader international tax and banking are subsequent regional expansion; additional currencies do not imply local tax compliance. Source passwords, active logins, lender approvals, private presentation layouts and unavailable export fields cannot be fabricated during migration: MOVE-001 to MOVE-009 require explicit mapping, retained history and an owner-reviewed disposition for each gap.
